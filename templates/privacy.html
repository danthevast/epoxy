YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026. This policy applies to the independent quote-matching website {{ site }}, serving commercial flooring projects in the St. Louis, Missouri area.

Who we are

{% if operator %}This site is operated by {{ operator }}. {% endif %}We operate a lead-matching service, not a flooring contractor. We review inquiries and may connect customers with independently operated commercial flooring contractors. We may receive a referral fee from participating contractors.

Information we collect

When you submit, we collect your project type, approximate area, ZIP code, timeframe, name, email and/or phone, optional details, submission time, and the consent wording you accepted. Do not send payment information or other sensitive personal information.

How we use and share an inquiry

We use this information to review project fit, communicate about your request, prevent duplicates and spam, and, after operator approval, share the inquiry with independently operated commercial flooring contractors serving your project area. Those contractors may contact you about this request. We do not automatically forward inquiries; an appropriate contractor agreement must be in place before sharing. Hosting and email providers process information only to support operation of the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

No marketing permission bundled into your request

The quote form does not authorize marketing calls, automated or prerecorded marketing calls, or marketing texts. We do not run those campaigns. If we later offer them, any legally required consent must be requested separately and cannot be a condition of requesting a match.

Cookies and measurement

We use a first-party session cookie for form security, admin access, and anonymous measurement. We count session visitors, form starts, completed inquiries, and qualification events for each landing-page version. The analytics database does not store names, email, phone, raw IP addresses, or third-party tracking identifiers. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals disable analytics. Counts are estimates, not unique people. No advertising trackers are installed.

Security and retention

Inquiry content is encrypted in our database and access to the dashboard is password-protected. Hashed contact fingerprints support duplicate detection; hashed connection identifiers support rate limiting. We retain inquiries for up to {{ retention }} days and analytics for 30 days through scheduled deletion. Backup copies may remain for up to 7 additional days. No security method is infallible.

Your choices and requests

You can decline to submit. You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop further sharing of your inquiry. We may need to verify your request. We cannot undo a disclosure already made; we can identify recipients and ask them to honor your request, subject to their obligations.
