GOOD QUESTIONS

Know what happens next.

Are you the company installing the floor?

No. We operate this quote-matching website. Any flooring work is quoted, contracted, and performed by an independent contractor you choose.

Does submitting a request cost anything?

No. There is no charge to submit a request and no obligation to hire. A contractor determines the price of any work. We may receive a fee from participating contractors for introductions; that does not guarantee a match.

Will my details be sent to contractors right away?

No. Our operator reviews every request. We only share with independently operated commercial flooring contractors serving your area after approval and once an appropriate contractor agreement is in place.

Can you guarantee a price, schedule, or contractor?

No. Site conditions and contractor availability vary. Confirm credentials, insurance, scope, pricing, and warranties directly before hiring.
